Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

The Saint Paul Board Game Group ("we," "us," or "our") operates this website to provide information about our board game community, events, venues, and ways to connect with us.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information necessary to operate and improve this website.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

If you contact us through our website, your submission is processed by Formspree. Depending on what you provide, this may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The contents of your message
  • Any other information you voluntarily include in your submission

We use this information to respond to inquiries, communicate with members and prospective members, and provide information you request.

Formspree may process and store submitted information in accordance with its own privacy practices and policies.

Information Collected Automatically

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website and to help us improve it.

Google Analytics may collect information such as:

  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of visits
  • Approximate geographic location
  • Browser and device information
  • Referring website
  • General information about how visitors interact with the website

This information is generally collected through cookies and similar technologies. We use the information for statistical and analytical purposes rather than to identify individual visitors.

Google may process information collected through Google Analytics in accordance with its own privacy policies.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies, including those associated with Google Analytics, to understand website traffic and usage.

You can generally control or disable cookies through your web browser's settings. You may also be able to limit Google's use of information through Google's available privacy controls.

Disabling cookies may affect certain website functionality.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses or may link to third-party services, including:

  • Formspree, which processes contact-form submissions
  • Google Analytics, which provides website analytics
  • Calendar and social media services that may be linked to or embedded on the website

These third-party services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those services when using their features.

How We Use Information

Information we collect may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and messages
  • Communicate with members and prospective members
  • Provide information about events and activities
  • Understand website usage and improve the site
  • Maintain and improve website security and functionality

We do not sell your personal information.

How We Protect Information

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through the website. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

How Long We Keep Information

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

The specific retention period may depend on the service through which the information was collected and the nature of the communication.

Your Choices

You may choose not to provide personal information through the website.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings and may use Google's available privacy controls to manage certain aspects of Google Analytics data collection.

If you have questions about information you have submitted or would like to request that we delete it, please contact the Saint Paul Board Game Group through the contact information provided on this website.

Children's Privacy

This website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently received personal information from a child under 13, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our website, services, or practices change. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle information, please contact the Saint Paul Board Game Group through the contact information provided on this website.
